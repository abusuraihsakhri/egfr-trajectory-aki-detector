eGFR Trajectory & AKI Detector

Review dated serum creatinine measurements against KDIGO 2012 AKI thresholds, urine-output criteria, and descriptive longitudinal eGFR slope. Runs entirely in the browser.

Research and education only. Not validated for clinical decision-making. Use de-identified data without personal identifiers.

Interpretation limits

    Missing comparator values cannot exclude AKI. A urine-output rate is treated as continuously sustained over the stated duration. eGFR slope during non-steady-state AKI is not evidence of chronic kidney disease progression.

    Based on KDIGO 2012 AKI criteria. No data is transmitted or persisted by this web application. Source and local Python API.
